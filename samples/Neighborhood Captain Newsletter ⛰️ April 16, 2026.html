Recovery Updates

  • STATE OF REBUILDING
    Over 1,000 homes have started construction, yet half of Eaton Fire survivors remain stalled by financial barriers and insurance delays.

  • WEED ABATEMENT & BRUSH CLEARANCE ASSISTANCE

    • Team Rubicon is helping neighbors clean up overgrown lawns in Altadena. Use this form to sign up.

    • Pasadena Jobs Center is helping hire out day laborers at $25/hour for brush and weed clearing. Call (626-440-0112) and speak with coordinator Juan.

  • TREES FOR ALTADENA
    Altadena Green has put together a list of recommended trees for Altadena. The list includes small, medium, and large trees. If you have an existing tree that needs watering, sign up for free tree watering support from Amigos de los Rios.

  • HELPING SMALL BUSINESSES
    The Altadena Chamber of Commerce has distributed $225,000 in gift cards to fire survivors as part of a $300,000 county-led milestone to support local businesses still struggling fifteen months after the Eaton Fire. Buy a gift card and get a 50% bonus gift card for free!

  • AUDITING SCE
    Fire survivors back Assembly Bill 1744 to mandate independent audits of Southern California Edison after reports revealed the utility failed to complete hundreds of millions of dollars in planned safety work while still charging customers.

  • AVOIDING CONTRACTOR SCAMS
    The Legal Aid Foundation of Los Angeles and Winston & Strawn LLP created a comprehensive guide to help disaster survivors safely hire contractors, review rebuild contracts, and avoid construction scams.

    • More legal resources: 

    • The Legal Aid Foundation of Los Angeles (LAFLA)

    • Bet Tzedek - Free Legal Services

  • WATER COMPANIES UPDATE
    On April 14, the Board of Supervisors backed two state bills meant to increase oversight of private mutual water companies, providing critical protections for Altadena residents served by the Lincoln Avenue, Las Flores, and Rubio Cañon water companies during fire recovery.

  • SCE OPENS REBUILD HUB
    SCE has opened Altadena Rebuild Community Hub (ARCH). Representatives will staff the ARCH to support customers with billing inquiries, planning inquiries (i.e. service reconnections, undergrounding, temporary power poles and panel), and other SCE programs and services.

    • Location: 2680 Fair Oaks Ave.

    • Hours: Monday-Friday, 8:00am-4:30pm

  • LA COUNTY CAPITAL IMPROVEMENT PLAN
    Watch the West San Gabriel Valley Area Capital Improvement Plan Kick Off - LA County’s  long-term strategic framework designed to prioritize, plan, and fund essential infrastructure—including mobility, water, and climate resilience.

  • NEW ALTADENA DEVELOPMENT PROJECTS
    On April 7, The Altadena Town Council Land Use Committee reviewed four West Altadena projects: the CORE community center at 2231 Lincoln Avenue, a 54-unit residential development at 2214 Windsor Avenue, an express car wash at Lincoln and Woodbury, and the redevelopment of Joe’s Mobile on West Woodbury Road. (Survey’s are still open, links below in Surveys)

  • ALL-ELECTRIC INCENTIVES, REBATES & GRANT
    You could get up to $26,900 (or more with an ADU or Passive House build) for building an all-electric home. These programs help pay for heat pumps, solar batteries, and induction stoves

    • California Electric Homes Program (CalEHP)

    • RISE Homes Program (Rebuilding Incentives for Sustainable Electric Homes)

    • Clean Power Alliance (Sun Storage Rebate

  • SCE UPDATES COMPENSATION PROTOCOLS
    Claimants represented by counsel at the time they submit a claim will now be compensated for 20% of net damages in a settlement offer to apply toward legal expenses. Settlement offers for tenants will now cover up to three months of housing assistance at the higher of actual pre-fire rental rates or monthly fair rental value.

    • Read SCE’s Press Release

Community & Financial Resources

SoCal Edison Wildfire Recovery Compensation Program | DEADLINE November 30, 2026

The Step Up Fund is giving $2,500 to fire survivors who face eviction.

Union Station Housing Assistance helps Eaton Fire survivors covering security deposits, rent, motel vouchers, and essential furnishings.

Pasadena City College Back in Business Fund is providing grants of $10,000-$20,000 for any small business owner impacted by the 2025 LA fires | DEADLINE April 30, 2026

RESTORE LA Fund for Micro & Small Business Recovery & Climate Resilience offers 3% no-fee loans of up to $100,000 to small businesses with revenue declines due to the 2025 LA wildfires.

Reach out to your Altagether Neighborhood Captain to hear about the Altagether Sherwin WIlliams paint discounts. Intended for neighbors/homeowners, not contractors.

The CalAssist Mortgage Fund Reopened and provides up to $100,000 in non-repayable mortgage assistance, covering up to 12 months of payments. Income limits are now up to $281,400 in LA County.

Disaster Case Management provides household support and is available through multiple organizations: 

  • CORE: dcmp@coreresponse.org 

  • LA County & Catholic Charities of California (FEMA-funded): (833) 775-DCMP (833-775-3267)

The LA County Sheriff’s Department Altadena Station’s Construction Check Program provides free, regular courtesy checks during the rebuilding process. Email altadenahomecheck@lasd.org with you and your contractor’s contact info.

The Fire Recovery and Resilience Workforce Program provides immediate hiring and training support for impacted and dislocated workers.
