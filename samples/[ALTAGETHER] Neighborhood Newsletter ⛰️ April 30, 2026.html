Recovery Updates

  • INSURANCE
    A delegation of fire survivors organized by EFSN fought for a suite of insurance reform bills in Sacramento, securing three major wins for consumer protection while facing a narrow defeat on fire-safe coverage.

  • PROPERTY TAX
    Two years of property tax are due today (April 30th). Individuals facing difficulties paying can file for individual penalty cancellations through the LA County Tax Collector’s office while advocates continue to push for further state-level deferment.

    • Penalty deferment form

  • DIRECT CASH ASSISTANCE FOR RENTERS
    Eligible Altadena renters displaced by the fire can apply for a first-in-the-nation direct cash assistance program providing $36,000 over two years, with applications open from May 11 - 25.

    • Click here for info and eligibility requirements (including having a dependent of any age)

    • Sign a letter of support for the initiative

  • $60,000 INTO FIRE SURVIVORS’ HANDS
    Local nonprofit My TRIBE Rise has distributed over $60,000 in direct cash grants and served thousands of residents through its mutual aid programs to support Altadena's working-class, elderly, and disabled survivors in the ongoing aftermath of the Eaton Fire.

  • E-CARGO BIKE VOUCHERS: $2,000 FOR PASADENA & ALTADENA RESIDENTS
    The San Gabriel Valley Council of Governments has announced $950,000 in new funding to provide $2,000 point-of-sale vouchers toward the purchase of electric cargo bikes. Apply at sgvcog.org/go-sgv

  • SCE CLAIMS AND TAXES UPDATE
    The U.S. House of Representatives passed the Doug LaMalfa Federal Disaster Tax Relief Certainty Act to make SCE settlement payments exempt from federal income tax; the bill now moves to the Senate before it can be signed into law.

  • SCE UPDATES COMPENSATION PROTOCOLS
    Claimants represented by counsel at the time they submit a claim will now be compensated for 20% of net damages in a settlement offer to apply toward legal expenses. Settlement offers for tenants will now cover up to three months of housing assistance at the higher of actual pre-fire rental rates or monthly fair rental value.

    • Read SCE’s Press Release

  • WILDFIRE CLAIMS TOP $500 MILLION
    Southern California Edison announced it has extended over $500 million in settlement offers to approximately 3,800 claimants, with its voluntary compensation program remaining open through November 30, 2026.

  • WILDFIRE SAFETY AND TESTING
    Assemblymember Harabedian’s bill to establish California’s first health-based standards for wildfire contamination testing and cleanup has cleared committee and now heads to Appropriations.

    • Add Your Support

    • Join EFRU on Monday, May 4 at 7 pm to discuss the status of AB 1642 and AB 1795

  • UNITED POLICYHOLDERS SURVEY
    The latest survey results reveal that 74% of respondents with standing homes remain displaced one year after the LA Fires. View Full Report

  • MORTGAGE FORBEARANCE
    AB1847 seeks to extend the Mortgage Forbearance Act by two additional years, specifically helping Eaton and Palisades fire survivors pause mortgage payments while they rebuild.

    • Altadenans can submit an official position letter through the California Legislative Position Letter Portal or contact the Appropriations Committee members directly.

  • ALTADENA TREE STORIES
    Join local journalist Mona Patterson and photographer Andrea Gaffney between May 14-17 in documenting the oral and visual history of the trees lost to or saved from the Eaton Fire.

    • Free, limited spots remain - Sign up here

  • WEED ABATEMENT & BRUSH CLEARANCE ASSISTANCE
    Pasadena Jobs Center is helping hire out day laborers at $25/hour for brush and weed clearing. Call (626-440-0112) and speak with coordinator Juan.

  • TREES FOR ALTADENA
    Altadena Green has put together a list of recommended trees for Altadena. The list includes small, medium, and large trees. If you have an existing tree that needs watering, sign up for free tree watering support from Amigos de los Rios.

  • HELPING SMALL BUSINESSES
    The Altadena Chamber of Commerce has distributed $225,000 in gift cards to fire survivors as part of a $300,000 county-led milestone to support local businesses still struggling fifteen months after the Eaton Fire. Buy a gift card and get a 50% bonus gift card for free!

  • AVOIDING CONTRACTOR SCAMS
    The Legal Aid Foundation of Los Angeles and Winston & Strawn LLP created a comprehensive guide to help disaster survivors safely hire contractors, review rebuild contracts, and avoid construction scams.

    • More legal resources:

    • The Legal Aid Foundation of Los Angeles (LAFLA)

    • Bet Tzedek - Free Legal Services

  • REBUILD ADVISORS
    PostFire is piloting a partnership that provides survivors with a free professional construction advisor for your entire build process. More information and to apply is available here.

  • ALL-ELECTRIC INCENTIVES, REBATES & GRANT
    You could get up to $26,900 (or more with an ADU or Passive House build) for building an all-electric home. These programs help pay for heat pumps, solar batteries, and induction stoves

    • California Electric Homes Program (CalEHP)

    • RISE Homes Program (Rebuilding Incentives for Sustainable Electric Homes)

    • Clean Power Alliance (Sun Storage Rebate)
