Recovery Updates

  • TODAY – Water Company Updates: Las Flores Water Company at 6 pm at the Main Library regarding a proposed $3,000 one-time fire recovery fee for shareholders. Rubio Canyon is considering a similar fee.

  • Deadline: TOMORROW! LA County Emergency Rent / Mortgage Relief Program applications are due January 23. There is financial relief for landlords whose tenants were unable to pay rent or for displaced homeowners who lost their homes in the fires and are on forbearance.

  • Watch the January 20, 2026 Altadena Town Council Meeting

    • 1:49: Director Norma Garcia-Gonzales of Parks and Recreation announces $60 million for Altadena parks

  • Soil Testing: FEMA will pay the EPA to test soil from 100 randomly selected sites in the Eaton Fire burn area. The soil samples would be collected near the surface and about 6 inches below ground. Test results are expected in April. More Info

  • Tax Accessors Office directive affirms their authority to interpret “substantially equivalent” to include up to a 110 percent threshold in square footage when valuing rebuilt properties in these fire impacted areas for tax purposes. This should help property owners who may need to modify the size or configuration of their homes to comply with updated building, fire, and environmental code standards.

  • SCE Legal Update: SCE is suing LA County and Pasadena Water and Power for shared blame in Eaton Fire.

  • Altadena for Accountability calls on Attorney General Bonta to investigate the Eaton Fire emergency response efforts.

  • Insurance Accountability: EFRU and LA Standing Homes (StandingHomes.org) are organizing a coordinated effort to submit insurer-specific letters to the Los Angeles County Office of the County Counsel, requesting investigations into potential bad-faith claims handling impacting standing homes after the January 2025 fires. Link and instructions on joining the letter.

  • The Altadena Library District Main Library will close for renovations on January 31, 2026 for ~18 months. The Bob Lucas and Loma Alta Branches will be open regular hours.

  • The Eaton Fire Area closure will continue into December 31, 2027.

  • SBA Reopening in person at the Collaboratory for those seeking assistance on the SBA application will now be available.

    • Where: The Collaboratory - 540 W Woodbury Rd.

    • When: Mon - Friday 9am - 6pm
